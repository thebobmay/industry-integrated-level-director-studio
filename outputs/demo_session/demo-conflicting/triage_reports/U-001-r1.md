## Diagnosis
The interpreted intent contains detected conflicts: the brief requests both 'beginner friendly' and 'hard' difficulty, as well as a 'relaxing' feel alongside elements that are not relaxing. These are incompatible and create ambiguity about the true design target.

## Tradeoff Reasoning
- A level cannot simultaneously be beginner-friendly and brutally hard, as these are mutually exclusive in terms of challenge and accessibility.
- A relaxing feel is at odds with constant enemies and huge gaps, which typically create tension and require focus.

## Independent Critic
- Verdict: approve
The Director correctly identified that the interpreted intent contains mutually exclusive goals: 'beginner friendly' and 'hard' difficulty, as well as 'relaxing' feel with non-relaxing elements. The candidate level itself is valid and easy, but the ambiguity in the brief makes it impossible to judge whether the level meets the true intent. Requesting clarification is the appropriate action before proceeding. No hard constraints are violated, and the diagnosis is consistent with the facts and intent.

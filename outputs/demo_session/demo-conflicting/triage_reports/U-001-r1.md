## Diagnosis
The interpreted intent contains detected conflicts: the brief requests both 'beginner friendly' and 'hard' difficulty with constant enemies and huge gaps, which are incompatible. It also requests a 'relaxing' feel alongside elements that create tension. These conflicting goals make it impossible to confidently judge the candidate against a clear target.

## Tradeoff Reasoning
- The brief's request for both beginner accessibility and hard difficulty with constant hazards is contradictory, as beginners require leniency and safe zones, while hard segments typically feature layered hazards and high challenge.
- A relaxing feel is at odds with constant enemies and huge gaps, which create tension and require precision.

## Independent Critic
- Verdict: approve
The Director correctly identified that the design brief contains irreconcilable conflicts: it simultaneously requests a beginner-friendly, relaxing segment and a hard, hazard-filled one, which are mutually exclusive. The candidate level is easy and relaxing, but this only satisfies part of the brief, and there is no clear way to judge it against the contradictory intent. The recommendation to request clarification is appropriate and does not overreach. No hard constraints are violated, and the diagnosis is consistent with the facts and intent.
